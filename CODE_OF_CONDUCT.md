# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Rookery contributors should make technical collaboration safe, direct, and useful. We welcome disagreement, careful criticism, and evidence that changes an earlier conclusion.

Expected behavior includes:

- treating people with respect across differences in experience, identity, and viewpoint;
- criticizing ideas and changes without attacking the person who proposed them;
- stating uncertainty, limits, and mistakes plainly;
- respecting privacy, event rules, authorization boundaries, and confidential reports;
- accepting a clear stop or scope correction immediately; and
- helping keep reviews focused on reproducible evidence and project impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, exposing private information, or using project access to target systems or people without authorization.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, review and discussion spaces, events organized under the project name, and private project communication. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through a private contact offered by the repository host or release announcement. If no private route is visible, request one publicly without describing the incident or identifying the people involved. Security-sensitive details should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to assess and address the report. They may remove content, restrict participation, reject contributions, or end project access when behavior threatens people, the project, or authorized event work. Anyone with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from handling the report.

Good-faith reports and respectful disagreement are not violations. Knowingly false reports, retaliation, and attempts to pressure a reporter are violations.

## Questions

Ask a maintainer privately when you are unsure how this policy applies. Preserve confidentiality while a concern is being reviewed.
